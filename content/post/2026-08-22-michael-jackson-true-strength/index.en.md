When you've been hurt or betrayed, it's natural to become less kind to others, or to think "I can't trust people anymore." The post calls that a very human reaction. But the more you learn about Michael Jackson, it says, the more one thing stands out: what happened to you and who you choose to be from here on are not necessarily the same thing.

---

## Someone Who Stayed Close to Others

The video opens with the fact that Michael was already visiting children's hospitals by the age of 14 — long before he became famous, he's portrayed as someone who reached out to others.

---

## He Carried Many Wounds of His Own

At the same time, Michael himself is said to have endured a harsh childhood, including strict corporal punishment from his father. The video also shows him visiting people suffering from illness and severe burns. Given all of that, the video suggests, it would have been understandable for him to close his heart off.

---

## No Matter How Many Times He Was Hurt, He Never Let Go of Kindness

And yet, rather than let his own pain become a reason to push people away, Michael turned it into a reason to reach out to them. The poster writes that this is where she senses his true strength.

The video closes with a message along the lines of: how you choose to be is something you get to decide for yourself.

---

## "I Can't" — Or "I Think I Can't"?

The post argues this isn't just about one extraordinary person — it applies to everyday life too. "I failed, so I'm not cut out for this." "I don't have confidence, so I can't." "It didn't work once, so it won't work again." Everyone has felt this way at some point. But is it really impossible — or do you just believe it's impossible? There's a real difference between the two.

Whatever happened in the past, and however things are going right now, you can still ask: "What can I do, starting from who I am today?" and "Who do I still want to be?" — that's the question the post leaves the reader with.

## Summary

* Losing trust in people after being hurt is a very human reaction
* Michael Jackson is said to have shown a habit of reaching out to others from an early age, visiting children's hospitals from age 14
* At the same time, he carried his own wounds — including strict corporal punishment from his father and the weight of visiting people suffering from illness and severe burns
* Even so, he never let his own pain become a reason to push people away, and kept turning it into kindness toward others instead
* What happened to you and who you choose to be are two different things — regardless of the past or the present, you can still choose what you do next and who you want to be, which is the message the video leaves the viewer with
